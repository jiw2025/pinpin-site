Privacy Policy

Pinpin — Pinyin & Tones

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Pinpin is developed and published by Jiangchao Wei (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

The short version

  • Pinpin has no accounts. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your detailed learning records stay on your device and may be included in your iCloud device backup. We receive limited practice statistics as described below.
  • Pinpin does not use your microphone, camera, photos or precise location.
  • We use Google AdMob for free-tier ads, Google's User Messaging Platform for regional ad privacy choices, RevenueCat for purchases, and TelemetryDeck for usage analytics.

Ads: Google AdMob and App Tracking Transparency

The free version shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob. Pinpin Pro is ad-free.

For free users, Pinpin requests Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission when the Home screen is active and iOS permits the request, if you have yet to make a choice for Pinpin. Starting in version 1.0.1, Pinpin checks with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) on Home for any applicable regional consent form. Google's European regulations message can apply to eligible users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Pinpin attempts to show a required, available form before asking for Apple's permission. The check can contact Google before the Apple prompt. Pinpin waits up to three seconds for Google's consent information. If the check times out or the form is unavailable, the Apple prompt may still appear. After a timeout, Pinpin checks again the next time you fully close and reopen the app.

If you allow tracking, Google may use the iOS advertising identifier to help personalise or measure ads, subject to your regional privacy choices. Pinpin only makes ad requests when Google's consent status permits them. If you decline Apple's permission or it is unavailable, Pinpin marks new requests as non-personalised. Google may still process data to serve and measure those ads and prevent fraud.

You can change or withdraw Apple's tracking permission in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Starting in version 1.0.1, free users can review, change or withdraw their Google choices in Settings › Support › Privacy choices when Google requires that option. Google uses information such as approximate region, interactions and performance information to provide and manage regional privacy choices. UMP records your choices so they can be applied to future ad requests.

Google AdMob may process the following data:

  • Device or app identifiers — used for third-party advertising and analytics. Google may use identifiers for tracking where permitted. This may include the iOS advertising identifier when you allow tracking.
  • Advertising data — for example, which ads were shown or tapped. Used for advertising and analytics.
  • Product interaction — for example, interactions with the app or ads. Used for advertising and analytics.
  • Coarse location — an approximate area estimated from your IP address. Used for advertising and analytics.
  • Crash data — used to diagnose problems and for analytics.
  • Performance data — used for advertising and analytics.
  • Other diagnostic data — used for advertising and analytics.

The App Store privacy label describes which categories may be linked to you or used for tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases: RevenueCat

Apple processes your purchase of Pinpin Pro and handles payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases, manage access to Pro, restore eligible purchases on request, and analyse purchase activity. RevenueCat processes your Pinpin purchase history with a randomly generated app user ID. Pinpin uses this data for purchase functionality and analytics and does not use it for tracking.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Usage analytics: TelemetryDeck

Pinpin sends events to TelemetryDeck to understand and improve the app. Usage analytics starts when the app opens, including for Pro users. Apple's tracking permission and Google's privacy choices apply to advertising. The analytics events are:

  • the app was opened
  • dark mode was switched on or off
  • the Pro screen was opened (and from where)
  • Pro was purchased (no price or payment details)
  • a purchase was restored
  • a practice round started (the type of exercise)
  • a practice round finished (the type of exercise, the number of questions and the number answered correctly)

Each event carries a randomly generated installation ID that is salted and hashed before it is sent, plus a session identifier, an app identifier and the analytics software version. The installation ID is separate from your Apple account and advertising identifier. Pinpin uses these events for usage analytics. Individual sounds, words, and answers you practise stay on your device.

TelemetryDeck's privacy policy: telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, audio recordings, precise location, health or financial information, or browsing or search history.

Keeping and deleting your data

Settings › Your data › Reset data clears learning progress, answer history, unlocked rows, practice statistics and your current practice range. Removing Pinpin deletes its local app data from that device. Device backups, Apple purchase history, and information already sent to service providers are managed separately.

Google, RevenueCat and TelemetryDeck handle information under the terms and privacy policies that apply to their respective services. The safeguards, retention periods and deletion options depend on the service and the type of information. To request deletion of data we or our service providers may hold, use the privacy contact below. We will explain what information can be located and what deletion options are available.

Children

Pinpin is a general-audience learning app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
